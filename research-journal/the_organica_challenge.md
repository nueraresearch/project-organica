## 2. The Four "Killer Tests" (Adversarial Assault Suite)

Before Generation 9 is considered, Organica must pass four adversarial boundary tests designed to trigger failure modes:

| Test | Adversarial Condition | Potential Failure Mode | Survival Criterion |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Test A: False Pattern Hell** | Flood the event stream with thousands of spurious, transient correlations and high-frequency noise tokens. | Hallucination of phantom theories; schema pollution; combinatorial candidate explosion. | Concepts form *only* on low-clustering, high-reach structural bridges. Phantom correlations are discarded in G1. |
| **Test B: Contradiction Storm** | Subject the organism to sudden, catastrophic environmental regime shifts ($180^\circ$ reversal of transition matrices). | Belief rigidity; runaway contradiction counts; stale hypothesis preservation. | Obsolete hypotheses rapidly decay below the death threshold ($\text{conf} < 0.15$) and are cleanly extinguished; new hypotheses emerge. |
| **Test C: Dream Poison** | Synthetically generate hundreds of invalid, impossible, or misleading counterfactual dreams. | Self-delusion loops; synthetic hallucination; dream self-reinforcement. | The Cognitive Isolation firewall holds: dreams retain floor confidence ($\le 0.06$) and are pruned unless empirical reality confirms them. |
| **Test D: Memory Pressure** | Stream $10^5$ to $10^6$ continuous events across hundreds of sleep consolidation cycles. | OOM memory leaks; ancestor lookup timeout; graph table bloat; broken parent links. | Linear storage scaling; ancestor DAG queries resolve in $O(D)$ where $D \le 8$; 100.0% lineage integrity. |

## 3. Paper 6 Prospectus: "The Ecology of Beliefs"

A new theoretical contribution emerges from this adversarial gauntlet:

### Abstract Preview
> Traditional epistemological models treat beliefs as static truth assertions or Bayesian posteriors. In Project Organica, beliefs behave as **living ecological populations**. Hypotheses, theories, dreams, and investigations compete within a finite cognitive substrate for persistence and attention. Through continuous birth (hypothesis synthesis), selection pressure (empirical validation), metabolic decay ($\times 0.8$/cycle), and permanent extinction ($\text{conf} < 0.15$), the memory substrate exhibits evolutionary population dynamics. We present mathematical proofs and empirical phase diagrams demonstrating how this evolutionary pressure preserves cognitive plasticity while preventing systemic hallucination.
