This directory preserves the complete development history of Project Organica — including the full design session transcript, every intermediate artifact, and the milestone reports produced as the architecture evolved from its first sleep cycle through Generation 8.

## Why This Matters

Most research projects publish only the final architecture. The development path — the failures, the dead ends, the moments where an invariant revealed itself — is discarded.

Project Organica preserves the full development record because **how the architecture evolved is part of the contribution**.

The self-contamination crisis (Discovery 2) led directly to the Cognitive Isolation Principle (Law III). The majority-class hallucination problem (Discovery 6) led directly to the EIG attention economy (Law V). These are not implementation details. They are the empirical evidence for why the design laws exist.
