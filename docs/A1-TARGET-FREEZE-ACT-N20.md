## 1. What is frozen

The act freezes **Path A** for the registered A1 attempt:

> the pre-37 cycle-membership directed-edge effect, **conditional on both an A carrier and a K path being present in the
> no-arm baseline**.

The conditional wording is the one [`DECISION-A1-COMPETENT-ESTIMAND`](DECISION-A1-COMPETENT-ESTIMAND.md) adopted; the
unconditional, representative-eligible wording is retired from this act and from every record that restates it.

It freezes the exact target strings in §2, taken from
[`a1_c8_target_selection_competent.json`](a1_c8_target_selection_competent.json), at **20 rows per seed where the
declared construction finds them and the largest block it finds otherwise, provided it reaches `N_min = 17`**. No
non-competent row is substituted to reach a size; a seed that cannot reach 17 supplies no block.

**C8's denominator follows the block, not a constant.** `floor(0.05 × N)` allows **one** assertion at twenty rows and
**none** at seventeen or eighteen, so seeds `20740102` (17) and `20740105` (18) must be perfect while the four
twenty-row seeds may spend one. The ceiling is unchanged at 0.05; what changed is the number of rows it is read over.

## 2b. The C1 universe this act restates

[`DECISION-A1-C1-CEILING`](DECISION-A1-C1-CEILING.md), adopted 2026-11-15, narrowed the `>64` clause to the *candidate-plan*
count it names and left the band's top to a comparison:

- **The scored denominator is every recorded paired-control row of the cell stream**, non-target edges included
  (`DECISION-A1-C1-DENOMINATOR-1`).
- **The preplanned universe is 112 candidate edges per seed**, recorded before treatment
  ([`a1_baseline_availability_adopted.json`](a1_baseline_availability_adopted.json)).
- **The ceiling is the comparison, not a constant:** a scored denominator larger than the preplanned universe is
  `VOID — design inconsistency`.
- **Observed minima:** 79 / 64 / 79 / 74 / 72 / 74 against the 60-row floor, met on all six seeds.

Adopting the competent block does not move this: C1 counts every recorded row, targets included, so the minima are a
property of the 112-row universe and the competent selection reproduces them exactly.

## 3. Effect of signature

Signing and dating this act did, in the freeze record:

- adopt the competent selection artifact and record its digest;
- set the manifest target block to `FIXED BEFORE FREEZE` with the exact `by_seed` mapping in §2;
- set `targets.n_per_seed` to **20** with `targets.n_min` to **17**, so the validator accepts a block between the floor
  and the nominal size;
- reserve the six candidate seeds as the fixed seed block;
- leave `authorisation.granted: false` and `granted_on: null`;
- leave the result absent and C1/C2/C4/C7/C8 unobserved.

The act does **not**:

- issue a grant;
- open a database or run a cell;
- produce a C1 or C4 denominator or a C8 measurement;
- regenerate targets or permit post-outcome re-selection;
- change the 0.05 C8 ceiling, the 0.05 C7 A/K margin, or the C1/C4 gate.

**The C1 denominator question is closed by record, not by this act.** The protocol sentence is authoritative
([`A1-TARGET-SELECTION-PROTOCOL.md`](A1-TARGET-SELECTION-PROTOCOL.md)), the floor applies to observed paired-control
denominators, and the 63–64 figure is a candidate-count upper bound. The ceiling is now the preplanned-universe
comparison in §2b.
