## What is measured

For each cycle: `new` edges entering the top-20 set from the previous cycle, `carried` edges kept from it, `returned` edges seen earlier but not in the previous cycle, `removed` edges dropped, and `jaccard` as the overlap share. `first_seen` counts edges entering the union, so the identity `slots = union + carried + returned` holds per seed. `first_seen_world` counts entries that are world edges; their sum is `S_ever`, the union of served world edges.

## The arithmetic, stated

`S_ever` = |top-20 union ∩ world| + served world cooccurrences. Across the six seeds that is 492 = 492 + 0: every served world edge is a transition, and 356 of the 848 union edges are not world edges. Turnover therefore counts churn that `S_ever` cannot see, while `S_ever` remains a subset of what the union has accumulated.
