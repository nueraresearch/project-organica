# Decision: progression beyond G8 is gated on activation, not on a G9 design

**Date:** 2026-11-14  
**Status:** `PLANNING DECISION — GENERATION 9 REMAINS LOCKED`  
**Scope:** roadmap and admission framing only. This document assigns no seed, authorises no cell, changes no engine file and records no result. **Naming:** G8/G9 here mean Generation 8 and Generation 9, not the emergence targets G1–G8 in `CHALLENGE.md`.

## Decision

Do **not** make “unlock Generation 9” the next objective. Make the next objective:

> **Determine what makes a developed, admitted or asserted structure behaviourally active, and whether that activation produces an external advantage.**

The question is registered as **Q056** in the research registry, as a future question blocked by Q054. The criteria, stage ledger and experiment ladder are in [`G9-ACTIVATION-DESIGN.md`](G9-ACTIVATION-DESIGN.md). Generation 9 — self-modeling — remains locked. It is not a placeholder for “everything not yet understood,” and no G9 design, layer or feature is authorised here. The question that must be answered first is the activation/use question, not the contents of a ninth generation.

## Why this framing

The project has real constraints, each within a tested scope:

- development occurs;
- development depends on history and curriculum;
- persistence affects candidate formation;
- some structures are asserted;
- some asserted edges are never served;
- carriers are enriched among asserted-but-never-served anomalies;
- admission and carrier formation are distinct processes;
- no single upstream selection stage has been identified.

Those constraints do not identify what activates a developed structure.

The available use-path evidence makes the gap concrete. The formation-versus-utilization campaign measured utilization losses with correct structure and mapped the next target to **retrieval / activation / readout** ([`formation_utilization.md`](formation_utilization.md)). The follow-up use-path diagnostic then failed its own D0 reproduction gate and is recorded as **AUDIT REQUIRED**, so it does not identify retrieval, activation or readout ([`USE-PATH-DIAGNOSTIC-RESULTS.md`](USE-PATH-DIAGNOSTIC-RESULTS.md)). C6 cleared temporal resolution, not activation ([`C6-TIMING-BLOCK.md`](C6-TIMING-BLOCK.md)). A0 found parallel admission and carrier paths, not a single selection stage ([`CARRIER-FORMATION-A0.md`](CARRIER-FORMATION-A0.md)).

The distinction that matters is:

| Quantity | What it means | Current status |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Formation** | A candidate structure exists | Supported within tested scopes |
| **Admission** | A world edge is eligible for service | A0 measures it; no single upstream stage |
| **Service** | The edge enters a served window | Measured, with 26 asserted-but-never-served edges |
| **Assertion** | The edge appears in the final structure | Measured; not the same as service |
| **Activation / use** | The structure changes behaviour or readout on a task | **Not established** |

A structure can be present and asserted without yet being shown to be behaviourally active. Collapsing these quantities into “selection” is what makes G9 look like the next step.

## The G8 → G9 gate

The progression gate is **X1 — External activation**, not a G9 design:

1. **A1 and M1 name a mechanism branch.** A1 eliminates at least one of carrier-causal, no-carrier-relation, shared-cause or readout, and M1 routes the target. A1 is currently `CONTRACT BUILT, NOT RUN`; no branch exists.
2. **A3 measures an external endpoint.** The endpoint is external to the internal assertion record and is fixed before the run.
3. **Ablation removes the advantage.** Removing or blocking the selected structure must remove the measured advantage. A score difference alone is not enough.
4. **Independent replication holds.** The result must survive an independent seed block or world family.
5. **Only then** is a Generation 9 question eligible. G9 still needs its own registered design and owner decision; passing X1 does not authorise it automatically.

If A3 is negative, `VOID`, or the ablation does not remove the advantage, the outcome is **`EXTERNAL ACTIVATION NOT ESTABLISHED`**. That closes the G8 → G9 path for the tested scope. It does not become a reason to design G9 anyway, add another endpoint, or reopen the same block.

## Stop condition

One A3 design and one registered run. No G9 design is written before X1 passes. A failed X1 is terminal for this path; a retry requires a new registered design with a named defect, not another instrumentation round. This is the same bounded-path rule used by the A1 gate layer.

## What this does not do

This is a planning decision, not evidence. It does not establish carrier causation, activation, transfer or external advantage. It does not assign seeds, authorise cells, change the engine or unlock Generation 9. It does not replace A1, M1 or A3; it orders them.
