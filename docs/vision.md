# Project Organica — Vision Statement

> *"Grow a digital infant rather than train an oracle."*

---

## Why Organica Exists

AGI research often begins with trillion-parameter architectures, massive compute clusters, and static datasets. In such systems, foundational cognitive questions remain hidden:
- Where do concepts come from?
- How is memory organically consolidated?
- How does an organism change its mind without catastrophic forgetting?
- How does curiosity originate without reinforcement-learning reward hacking?

When intelligence is pre-baked into trillions of static weights, we cannot observe how understanding actually grows.

Project Organica takes the opposite stance: **Complexity is the enemy of discovering principles.**

We build the smallest possible substrate capable of open-ended developmental growth.

---

## The Four Foundations

### 1. No Foundation Model Initially
The reasoning model is not the organism; the **memory system** is the organism. Intelligence is an emergent property of structural memory consolidation across time.

### 2. Epistemology Over Text
The system does not write English summaries of what it learned. It forms graph nodes:
- `ConceptCandidate`
- `ProcessCandidate`
- `HypothesisCandidate`
- `QuestionCandidate`
- `DreamCandidate`
- `InvestigationCandidate`

Knowledge is structural, computable, and testable.

### 3. Falsifiability and Selection Pressure
Without death, memory becomes a landfill. Without contradiction, belief becomes dogmatism. Organica subjects every candidate node to selection pressure:
- Unconfirmed structures decay exponentially.
- Falsified hypotheses face permanent pruning (extinction).
- Structural conflict generates questions (curiosity).

### 4. Active Investigation
Once an organism can hold competing hypotheses and generate synthetic dreams, it moves from passive observation to an active scientist: ranking uncertainties by **Expected Information Gain (EIG)** and preparing active probes.
