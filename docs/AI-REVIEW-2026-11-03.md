### 2. The simulation does not settle the 90 % threshold — VALID

> "Those are point estimates on opposite sides of the bar, but their Monte Carlo uncertainty means the simulation

### 3. The endpoint gate still rests on an unjustified scale and precision — VALID, blocking

> "The claimed 0.05 effect gap is still derived as `2 × 0.50 × 0.05`; a Spearman correlation and assumed outcome SD
> alone don't establish that log-loss difference without additional assumptions. The proposed per-world SE,
> `s/√N`, also isn't demonstrated by the current scorer: `score()` returns aggregate scores, not per-world SEs, and
> its loss contributions are nested over contexts, source tags, and vocabulary—not obviously independent. The

Confirmed. `score()` accumulates over every context source crossed with the vocabulary and returns a single rounded

per-world standard error exists in the scorer. The `√N` substitution is withdrawn; the gap now carries its three
conditions; the granularity gate reads **open** rather than informed, with the precision report named as the
measurement that would discharge it.
