# Decision: A1 is committed to target resolution, not left unfrozen

**Date:** 2026-11-14

**Status:** `ADOPTED — COMMITTED TO TARGET RESOLUTION; NO RUN AUTHORISED`

**Scope:** program disposition only. This document assigns no seed, freezes no manifest, authorises no cell, changes no engine file and records no result.

## Decision

A1 is **committed to target resolution**. The project will produce and review a pre-outcome target-selection protocol, fix the per-seed target edges, freeze the manifest against those targets, and then present a dated run-authorising grant decision to the owner. This is a commitment to the preparation path, not to a run.

The current A1 manifest remains `REGISTERED DESIGN — NOT FROZEN, NOT RUN, NO GRANT`; its target edges remain unresolved; its seed block remains provisional; and every executing path continues to refuse before a database is opened. No cell is authorised by this decision.

## Why

The full suite is green and the governance, registry and decision-index repairs are stable. The remaining A1 blockers are no longer CI, documentation, decision indexing or validator wiring. They are unresolved per-seed targets, an unfrozen manifest and no dated grant. Leaving the design in that state neither produces evidence nor closes the mechanism-discrimination question. A1 is the first registered test that can eliminate the carrier-causal, no-carrier-relation, shared-cause or readout paths; A3/X1 and any V2 discussion remain downstream.

## What this does not do

It does not freeze the manifest, reserve the candidate seeds, authorise any cell, issue a grant, clear C1–C9, record a mechanism or produce an A1 result. C6 remains the only satisfied gate; the other eight remain contract-built but unrun. A grant is a separate owner act, and the manifest gate must still refuse until the target sets, freeze and grant are all recorded.

## Revisit condition

This disposition is revisited at the earlier of:

- the target-selection protocol and per-seed target sets being ready for a freeze review; or
- the owner declining the A1 grant, or the project declaring that it cannot produce pre-outcome targets under the manifest rule.

If no target-selection protocol is recorded and no dated grant decision is made by **2026-12-14**, this disposition lapses to **A1 PARKED** by default, with the reason and revisit condition recorded. The date is a default review point, not a seed allocation or a run date.

## Rejected alternatives

- **Park A1 now.** Rejected: the remaining blockers are concrete and bounded; parking before attempting target selection would close the question without using the built gate and arms.
- **Freeze and run A1 without fixed targets.** Rejected: a frozen manifest with an empty target set cannot discriminate the two paths and would spend the registered attempt on an uninterpretable run.
- **Move directly to A3 or V2 while A1 stays unfrozen.** Rejected: A3/X1 is downstream of A1/M1 routing; V2 is reserved for a mechanism-driven redesign after M1 and G4.

## What would change this

A target-selection protocol that cannot identify per-seed targets before outcomes, or an owner decision that resources or risk do not support the first run, flips the disposition to **A1 PARKED** rather than another instrumentation round.
