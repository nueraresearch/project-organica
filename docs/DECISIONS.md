### DECISION-ENGINE-EFFECT-STANDARD — Decision: what an engine-effect claim must rest on

**Status.** ADOPTED — *"An engine-effect claim rests on a single-variable arm, a same-build control, a shipped-build reproduction and a live seed axis."*

**Decided.**

- A treatment that changes one engine constant and nothing else is measured in process first, against the shipped arm on the same seeds.
- The control is the same build the claim names: the pre-repair behaviour is measured, not inferred from an earlier version.
- The repair is reproduced with the shipped engine at the new version, end to end.
- The seed axis must be live: distinct per-seed trajectories, or the block is a single case wearing a seed count.
- Gates are fixed before the run, and a block that fails its own preflight gate is VOID.
- A VOID block may not be cited as confirmation and may not be cited as a failed replication; it measured nothing about the engine.
- The claim's build is derived from artifact stamps, and a chain that spans builds says so instead of naming one of them.
- Promotion into the engine is a separate owner decision with its own date, its own record and a version bump.

- *Cite the VOID replication arm as a failed replication.* — because *"A block that fails its own preflight gate measured nothing about the engine, and reporting it as a failed replication turns an inadmissible run into evidence."*
- *Let the claim's engine version be the one the author remembers.* — because *"The build is derived from artifact stamps; a chain that spans builds must say so, and a mixed field is the honest record."*

### DECISION-A1-C1-CEILING — Decision: the C1 ceiling is the preplanned universe, not the number 64

**Status.** ADOPTED — *"ADOPTED BY OWNER 2026-11-15 — THE 63–64 CLAUSE GOVERNS THE CANDIDATE-PLAN COUNT; THE CEILING IS THE PREPLANNED UNIVERSE; IN FORCE"*

**Decided.**

- The `>64` clause is narrowed to the quantity it names — the 63–64 candidate-plan count — and does not govern the operative cell-row denominator that `DECISION-A1-C1-DENOMINATOR-1` adopted.
- The ceiling's function is carried by a comparison rather than a constant: the scored denominator must equal the preplanned universe recorded in the freeze act, and any denominator larger than that universe is `VOID — design inconsistency`.
- The preplanned universe is restated from the pre-treatment baseline artifact before either act is signed, and the restated value is written into the act it freezes.

**Rejected.**

- *Read the clause literally and accept five seeds as VOID* — because *"It voids the adopted block before any retention is read, for a denominator that is exactly the preplanned one; and it would still pass a 64-row run that added four rows after the baseline, so it fires where nothing is wrong and stays silent where the defect is."*

- *Delete the ceiling and rely on the instruction to score preplanned rows* — because *"A check that must be remembered is not a check: with no comparison against the recorded universe, a scored population larger than the planned one is invisible."*
- *Treat the narrow reading as already settled by the checklist footnote* — because *"A documentation edit cannot decide a binding record, and that footnote has been retracted; the clause needs a record of its own."*

## What this graph is not

- **Not a rationale.** The reasoning lives in the document. This index quotes what it says
  and does not summarise it, because a summary is where a decision quietly becomes the
  version that turned out to be convenient.
- **Not a ranking.** A PENDING decision is not more important than an ADOPTED one; the
  statuses say whether the choice is in force, not whether it was right.
- **Not a re-scoring.** Withdrawing or keeping a decision is the owner's act. This page
  reports the status each document states, quoted.
