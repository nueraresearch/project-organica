## Abstract
Generative models frequently suffer from hallucination loops when trained or consolidated on their own synthetic outputs. Yet biological organisms use sleep-state replay and dreaming to simulate counterfactual realities and explore unobserved behavioral strategies. This paper introduces the **Grounded Dreaming Framework** within Project Organica. By constraining dream synthesis to four discrete topological transformations (*completion, analogy, mutation, and conditional context separation*) and enforcing the invariant that dreams never directly reinforce beliefs, Organica explores vast combinatorial spaces while maintaining epistemic immunity to self-delusion.

---

## 1. Dream Taxonomy
1. **Completion Dreams**: Infer transitive shortcuts across validated causal edges ($A \rightarrow B, B \rightarrow C \implies \text{Dream } A \rightarrow C$).
2. **Analogy Dreams**: Graft structural equivalents across high co-occurrence synonyms.
3. **Mutation Dreams**: Extend proven workflows with unvisited meta-concept anchors.
4. **Conditional Dreams**: Isolate unmodeled context tags capable of resolving high-entropy questions.

---

## 2. Epistemic Immunity Protocol
To prevent generative feedback loops:
- Initial Dream Confidence: Clamped to $\text{conf}_0 \in [0.04, 0.06]$.
- Initial Novelty: Set to $\mathcal{N} \in [0.70, 0.90]$.
- Belief Invariant: Dreams are strictly treated as **untested hypotheses**. Only empirical feedback generated by external experiments ($G_8$) can validate a dream into an accepted belief.
